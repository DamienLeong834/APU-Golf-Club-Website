What We Offer

Everything you need to get started with golf on campus, from coaching to tournaments and community socials.

Professional Coaching

Weekly group and private lessons led by experienced coaches to improve your swing, putting and course strategy.

Tournaments & Leagues

Compete in friendly inter-club and university matches, with prizes, rankings and plenty of fun along the way.

Community & Networking

From social days to industry talks with golf professionals, build friendships and career connections for life.

Who we are

Our Mission

The APU Golf Club exists to make golf accessible, affordable and fun for all students through community, coaching and career networking.

Our club is led by President Leong Yuan Kang and advised by Ahmad Mohammad, and we welcome players of every skill level — from absolute beginners to seasoned golfers.

Read Our Story

Vision

To grow a vibrant golfing community at APU and represent the university with pride within inter-university competition.

Values

Accessibility, sportsmanship, teamwork, integrity and lifelong learning on and off the fairway.
