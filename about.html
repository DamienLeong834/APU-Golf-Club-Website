About the APU Golf Club

Our mission, vision, values and the people who make it happen.

Who we are

Our Story

The Asia Pacific University Golf Club (APUGC) is a student society creating a friendly, welcoming golfing community across campus.

Our Mission

To make golf accessible, affordable and fun for all students through community, coaching and career networking. We break down the barriers of cost and experience so everyone can enjoy the game.

Our Vision

To grow a vibrant golfing community at APU and represent the university with pride in inter-university competition, while nurturing confident, well-rounded student leaders.

Our Objectives

  • Provide weekly coaching for all skill levels
  • Organise tournaments and friendly matches
  • Host social and networking events
  • Build career connections with golf professionals
Our values

What We Stand For

Accessibility

Golf for everyone, regardless of budget or experience.

Teamwork

We grow together, on and off the fairway.

Integrity

We play by the rules with honesty and respect.

Excellence

Always improving our game and our club.
