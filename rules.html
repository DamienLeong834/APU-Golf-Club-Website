Rules & Regulations

Play the game right

The Essential Rules of Golf

A beginner-friendly guide to the most important rules, in line with the official Rules of Golf.

RuleDescription
Teeing OffTee the ball within the teeing area, between and behind the tee markers. Play from outside this area results in a penalty.
Play the Ball as It LiesDo not move, bend or break anything fixed or growing. Play the ball from where it comes to rest without improving your lie.
Out of BoundsIf the ball goes out of bounds or is lost, take a penalty stroke and re-drop under penalty (stroke and distance).
Water HazardsFor a ball in a penalty area, drop outside with a one-stroke penalty, keeping the point of entry between you and the hole.
Order of PlayThe player farthest from the hole plays first. On the tee, the honour goes to the player with the best score on the previous hole.
Count Every StrokeRecord every stroke including penalty strokes. Failure to include strokes results in disqualification in competition.
On the course

Etiquette & Safety

Safety First

  • Never swing when others are close by.
  • Shout “Fore!” if a ball might hit someone.
  • Do not walk in front of another player taking a shot.
  • Stay clear of the green while others are putting.

Consideration

  • Maintain a good pace of play; keep up with the group ahead.
  • Rake bunkers after use and repair divots and ball marks.
  • Keep the course tidy and dispose of litter properly.
  • Be respectful to fellow players and course staff.
Note: Full, official rules are governed by The R&A and USGA. Club tournaments follow the standard Rules of Golf with any local rules announced before play.
Club conduct

APU Golf Club Code of Conduct

  • All members must register for events and observe safety briefings.
  • Respect the dress code and required equipment on the course.
  • Treat teammates, opponents and staff with courtesy at all times.
  • Represent the club and APU positively in inter-university events.
  • Report any injuries or incidents immediately to the committee.
  • Pay membership fees promptly and support club activities.
