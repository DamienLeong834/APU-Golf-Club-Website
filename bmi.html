Understanding BMI

What is BMI?

BMI is a measurement that estimates body fat based on height and weight. It’s a useful screening tool, though it doesn’t directly measure body fat.

BMI Scale:

BMI Categories for Adults:

  • Underweight: BMI less than 18.5
  • Normal weight: BMI 18.5 to 24.9
  • Overweight: BMI 25 to 29.9
  • Obesity: BMI 30 or greater
Note: BMI is a general indicator and may not apply to athletes, elderly, or pregnant individuals. For personalised health advice, consult a professional.

Source & Copyright: The Body Mass Index (BMI) formula and the BMI classification categories (<18.5, 18.5–24.9, 25–29.9, 30+) used in this calculator are based on the standards published by the World Health Organization (WHO). BMI is defined as a person’s weight in kilograms divided by the square of their height in metres (kg/m²).
