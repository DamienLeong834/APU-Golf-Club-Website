Attire & Equipment

What you need to get on the course.

Dress the part

Required Attire

Most courses enforce a dress code. Follow these guidelines to play without issues.

Tops

  • Collared polo or golf shirt
  • Sleeveless tops must have a collar
  • No denim, singlets or t-shirts on course

Bottoms

  • Smart shorts, trousers or skorts
  • Chinos or golf pants preferred
  • No jeans, tracksuits or cargo pants

Footwear

  • Golf shoes or soft-spiked trainers
  • Clean, non-marking soles
  • No open-toed shoes or sandals
Gear up

Essential Equipment

A standard golf bag holds up to 14 clubs. Beginners can start with a basic set.

EquipmentPurposeNote for Beginners
Driver & WoodsLong-distance shots from the tee and fairwayA 5-wood is easier for beginners than a driver.
Irons (5–9)Approach shots of varying distanceA basic 7-iron is the most versatile starting club.
WedgesShort, high shots around the greenPitching wedge is essential; sand wedge for bunkers.
PutterRolling the ball on the greenChoose a putter that feels comfortable to you.
Golf BallsThe ball you play with each holeBring several spare balls on your first few rounds.
Golf BagCarrying and protecting your clubsA lightweight stand bag suits beginners.
Tees & MarkersTeing the ball and marking your ball on the greenCarry a small supply in your pocket.
Tip: The club can arrange club rental and starter sets for beginners. Ask the committee at apugolfclub@apu.edu.my before you buy anything expensive.
Handy extras

Nice-to-Have Items

  • Golf gloves & rain gear for playing comfort.
  • Sun protection: cap, sunscreen and sunglasses.
  • A towel and brush to keep clubs clean.
  • A range finder or golf app for distances (optional).
  • Water bottle and light snacks for long rounds.
